AI workflow

AI builds fast. I decide what ships.

AI agents write much of my code and generate many of my assets. My job is the direction and the review: I play every build, find what is wrong, and fix it or send it back.

  1. Brief

    Scope, flow, art direction and the one feeling the ad has to leave. I draft with ChatGPT, then edit it myself.

  2. Build with agents

    Claude Code writes TypeScript and shaders in the repo, one commit per milestone. Through the Blender MCP it also models the 3D assets.

  3. Play and review

    I play every build on a phone and on desktop, at every aspect ratio, and check the console, the frame rate and the build size.

  4. Fix and own

    I send back what feels wrong, fix art by hand when that is faster, and can explain every line that ships.

A "z-fight" that wasn't one

I spotted flickering stripes on the building windows. The cause was interpolation noise amplified by a hash in the shader, so the fix was flat varyings, not a depth offset.
